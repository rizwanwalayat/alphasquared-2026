Trucking & Transport · Alliance Driveaway 350+ drivers in sync
Problem
Loads were coordinated by phone and spreadsheet across a continent, so the office and the driver rarely agreed on where a truck actually was.
Solution
One dispatch board plus native iOS and Android driver apps, with photo, GPS and signature chain of custody and a QuickBooks sync from load to invoice.
Result
350+ drivers across 48 states and Canada on one system, in production and still shipping.

Public Safety · Safe City 3 government systems
Problem
Agencies held their data in systems that were never built to talk to each other, and the moments it mattered most were the moments nobody had time to reconcile three databases by hand.
Solution
A data exchange system, a digital forensics system with evidentiary chain of custody, and a media monitoring system, all under one role-based access model.

Training & Simulation · Coca-Cola 0 hours of warehouse downtime
Problem

Solution
A 3D warehouse simulation with realistic handling, scenario-based hazard training and performance assessment before anyone touches a real machine.
Result
Trainees repeat the dangerous scenarios safely, and no shift stops for a training session.
