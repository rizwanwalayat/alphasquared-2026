## 2. Featured Case Studies (detailed — from the Development Portfolio deck)

These 10 have full descriptions + tech stacks and are the strongest candidates for a "Work" / "Case Studies" page.
Images for each are in the accompanying zip (`alpha-squared-project-images.zip`) — filename noted under each project.

### Oravia — Wellness in Tourism
**Image:** `01-oravia.jpg`
Certification platform for authentic wellness in the travel and tourism industry. Ensures travel suppliers meet standards for wellness properties, benefiting travelers and local communities. Partners include Department of Culture & Tourism, Florida Chamber of Commerce, Las Vegas Tours Co., Tour Turkey Co.
**Category:** Travel / Tourism Certification

### Alkanooz — Homepage & E-commerce
**Image:** `02-alkanooz-homepage.jpg` (alt version: `39-alkanooz-alt.jpg`)
Home décor e-commerce brand for handcrafted metal and wood items, drawing on artisan heritage. Site includes shop, lookbook, blog/press, and Instagram integration.
**Category:** E-commerce

### Amwalouna — Real Estate System
**Image:** `03-amwalouna.jpg`
Real estate management system to manage property sales and customer relationships — customer records, purchased units, contracts, customized payment plans with automated installment tracking, and full visibility into project/unit/contract/payment status.
**Tech:** .NET, SQL Server

### Compo++ — Oil & Gas Software (ADNOC)
**Image:** `04-compo-plus-plus.jpg`
AI-powered platform for the oil and gas industry, transforming raw fluids characterization data into structured, actionable insights. Supports data digitization, ingestion, QA/QC, visualization, and exploratory analysis. Includes an Agentic AI assistant combining physics-backed models with predictive guidance.
**Tech:** Go, Python, Vue.js, MySQL

### Fluidsdata — Energy / Oil & Gas SaaS
**Image:** `05-fluidsdata.jpg`
AI-powered SaaS platform for oil & gas, same core capability as Compo++ (data digitization, QA/QC, visualization, exploratory analysis, Agentic AI assistant). Serves major clients including Chevron, ExxonMobil, and ADNOC.
**Tech:** Go, Python, Vue.js, MySQL

### Repair Beat — Fitness / Healthcare SaaS
**Image:** `06-repair-beat.jpg`
Cloud-based SaaS POS and operations management platform for mobile/electronics repair businesses. Multi-tenant architecture with repair ticketing, technician assignment, inventory tracking, multi-location stock transfers, role-based access. Integrates PayPal and Stripe, an embeddable lead-capture widget, built-in CMS, and subscription admin panel — comparable to RepairDesk.
**Tech:** PHP, Laravel, Vue.js, MySQL

### FoodStretcher Plus & OCR — Fintech / Consumer Rewards
**Image:** `07-foodstretcher-plus-ocr.jpg`
Consumer rewards platform where shoppers earn cash-back on eligible grocery purchases — onboarding, receipt submission, rewards tracking, payout workflows across web and mobile. Features a custom-built OCR microservice for automated receipt scanning and validation, built with a focus on accessibility for unbanked/benefits-dependent users.
**Tech:** PHP, Laravel, React Native

### Kingsland — Real Estate System (Pakistan)
**Image:** `08-kingsland.jpg`
Full-featured real estate listing and management platform for the Pakistani market. Public-facing property listing portal (browse/filter/inquire) plus Admin and Agent dashboards for listings, leads, agent performance, and client communications. Web built on WordPress; native Flutter apps for Android/iOS.
**Tech:** WordPress, PHP, Flutter

### Alliance Driveaway Solutions — Real Estate System (label as shown; actually logistics)
**Image:** `09-alliance-driveaway-solutions.jpg`
Complete digital ecosystem for a North American vehicle relocation company operating across all 48 U.S. states and Canada. Public website, internal web dashboard, and native iOS/Android apps connecting 350+ drivers, clients, and operations. Real-time dispatch, driver management, job tracking, client communication for heavy/Class 6-8 truck relocation. Built to Small Woman-Owned Business (SWOB) standards.
**Tech:** PHP, Laravel, Vue.js, Kotlin, Swift

### Who I Follow — Sports Social Platform
**Image:** `10-who-i-follow.jpg` (alt versions: `14-who-i-follow-alt-a.jpg`, `15-who-i-follow-alt-b.jpg`)
Sports social and event management platform for leagues/associations, starting with softball. Manages teams, divisions, parks, umpires, schedules, and large tournaments (hundreds of teams). Automated pool play, bracket generation, umpire assignments, real-time scoring via mobile. Live leaderboards, public event views, encrypted chat, notifications.
**Tech:** PHP, Laravel, Vue.js, MySQL, Kotlin, Swift
