The short version

Four Commitments That Never Move

Everything further down this page is the detail behind these four.

Ownership

You Own the Code

Source, IP and infrastructure definitions transfer to you on final payment, in a repository you control. No lock-in and no hostage repositories.

Jurisdiction

Canadian Company, Canadian Paper

Incorporated in Alberta, contracting under Canadian law, PIPEDA applies to us by default. Data residency is chosen with you, not assumed.

Access

Least Privilege, Always

Credentials are scoped per client and per environment, never shared across projects, and revoked the week an engagement ends.

Accountability

Targets in Writing First

Uptime, response times and escalation paths are agreed before go-live, sized to your system rather than pasted from a template.

Security in practice

Controls We Apply on Every Engagement

Not a certification badge. The specific things we do on your system, whether the contract is six weeks or six years.

Application

  • Secrets kept out of source control, injected at deploy
  • Parameterised queries and input validation at every boundary
  • Dependency and vulnerability scanning in the pipeline
  • Auth, session and role checks reviewed before merge

Infrastructure

  • Encrypted in transit end to end, encrypted at rest
  • Staging and production kept as separate environments
  • Automated backups with a restore that gets tested
  • Logging and alerting wired up before launch, not after

People & Process

  • Named engineers on your project, under NDA
  • Every change peer-reviewed before it merges
  • Access granted per environment and revoked at handover
  • Incident response path agreed with you in advance

The paperwork

Five Documents, All of Them Yours to Keep

None of it is invented mid-project and none of it is chased by hand. Every document is generated from the same scope record the build runs against, so versions never drift and nobody retypes a number between them. Send your legal team's paper instead of ours wherever you'd rather; we work either way.

01

Mutual NDA

Signed before we go into specifics about your system, your data or your roadmap.

02

Master Services Agreement

Liability, IP assignment and termination, settled once instead of per phase.

03

Statement of Work

Scope and price per phase, written down, with the change process agreed up front.

04

Service Level Agreement

Uptime, response times and escalation, sized to your system and signed before go-live.

05

Handover Pack

Source, docs, credentials and the automation runbook, delivered on final payment.
