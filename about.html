The short version

Started as one designer-developer.
Grew into a studio, not a staffing pool.

Alpha Squared began in 2012 in Edmonton, Alberta, building web and mobile products for clients who needed one accountable partner rather than a rotating cast of contractors. Fourteen years and 265+ projects later, that's still the model: a senior engineering bench assembled per engagement, led by a named principal who answers for the whole build. Trucking and transport became our flagship vertical after we built the digital ecosystem behind Alliance Driveaway Solutions — and AI moved from experiment to production the same way everything else here does, by shipping it for a real client first.

How we work

The same four steps,
every single time.

No surprises in the process, because the process doesn't change based on how the last project went.

STEP 01

Discover

Ride-along process mapping and fixed-price scoping, so we understand the operation before a line of code exists.

STEP 02

Design

Field-tested UX and a clickable prototype first — you see and approve the shape of it before we build.

STEP 03

Build

Senior engineers shipping working software weekly. Demos, not status reports; native iOS, Android and web.

STEP 04

Operate

Monitoring, real SLAs and roadmap ownership. We're still here after launch, when it counts most.
