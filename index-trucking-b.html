Why nothing off the shelf fits

Your operation breaks every assumption a TMS makes.

Which is why driveaway companies run on spreadsheets, email and text messages — or pay to build in-house. There is no product to displace.

01 — NO TRAILER

The asset being moved is the cargo.

No trailer, no bill of lading in the normal sense. Every load screen in every TMS starts from the wrong object.

02 — NO ELD

It's the customer's vehicle.

Often no telematics and nothing to pull from. Tracking has to be phone-based, which makes the driver app the tracking system.

03 — NO ROSTER

Drivers are a 1099 network.

Not employees on a route. They accept or decline, they're spread across a continent, and their qualification status is yours to prove.

04 — NO ROUND TRIP

Every job is one-way.

Creating a deadhead and repositioning problem with no analogue in freight — and therefore no solution in freight software.
