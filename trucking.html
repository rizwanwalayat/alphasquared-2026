Why nothing off the shelf fits

Your Operation Breaks Every Assumption a TMS Makes

That's why driveaway companies run on spreadsheets, email and text messages. There is no product to displace.

No trailer

The Asset Being Moved Is the Cargo

No trailer, no bill of lading in the normal sense. Every load screen in every TMS starts from the wrong object.

No ELD

It's the Customer's Vehicle, Not Yours

Often no telematics and no ELD to pull from. Tracking has to be phone-based, which means the driver app is the tracking system.

No roster

Drivers Are a Distributed 1099 Network

Not employees on a route. They accept or decline, they're spread across a continent, and their qualification status is yours to prove.

No round trip

Every Job Is One-Way

Which creates a deadhead and repositioning problem with no analog in freight, and therefore no solution in freight software.
