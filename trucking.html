Why nothing off the shelf fits

Your operation breaks
every assumption a TMS makes.

That's why driveaway companies run on spreadsheets, email and text messages — or pay to build in-house. There is no product to displace.

No trailer

The asset being moved is the cargo.

No trailer, no bill of lading in the normal sense. Every load screen in every TMS starts from the wrong object.

No ELD

It's the customer's vehicle, not yours.

Often no telematics and no ELD to pull from. Tracking has to be phone-based, which means the driver app is the tracking system.

No roster

Drivers are a distributed 1099 network.

Not employees on a route. They accept or decline, they're spread across a continent, and their qualification status is yours to prove.

No round trip

Every job is one-way.

Which creates a deadhead and repositioning problem that has no analogue in freight — and no solution in freight software.

What we build

Six pieces, built for this operation
and nobody else's.

Dispatch board

Job creation to delivery in one live view. Assign, reassign and see status without a single back-and-forth email.

Native driver app

iOS and Android, offline-tolerant. Accept or decline jobs, capture condition photos and signatures, submit documents, see pay status.

Deep QuickBooks sync

Load to invoice without anyone retyping it. Payment sync-back and chart-of-accounts mapping — not a push-only checkbox.

Chain of custody

Photo, GPS and signature stamped on every handoff. A tamper-evident record of who had a $180,000 truck, and when.

Driver qualification

Every driver's status provable before the roadside inspection — not reconstructed from a folder after one.

Intelligent assignment

Matching contractor drivers to one-way jobs across driver location, deadhead cost, endorsements and reliability history.

Dispatch, driver apps and QuickBooks are live in production today. Live truck tracking and intelligent assignment are in build.
