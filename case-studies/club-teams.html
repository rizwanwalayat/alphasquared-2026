Sports Tech

What it is

A sports club and team management platform covering player profiles and performance tracking. Built on the same understanding of amateur sports administration that produced Who I Follow.

PHP / LaravelVue.jsMySQL

The problem

Club coaching turns over constantly, and every departing coach takes their assessment of a player's development with them. The club retains the player and loses the history, which makes long-term development mostly guesswork.

What we shipped

  • Player profiles that persist across seasons and coaches
  • Performance tracking against recorded metrics
  • Team and squad management
  • Club-level visibility across all teams

Where it stands today

  • Player history survives a coaching change
  • Development is measured over seasons, not recalled
  • Club administrators see across all teams at once
  • Shares design lineage with our Who I Follow platform
