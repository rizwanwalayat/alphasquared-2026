AI in production

What it is

A voice agent that picks up the phone, works out what the caller wants, checks the real calendar, offers times that actually exist, takes the booking and writes the whole interaction into HubSpot. The caller does not get an IVR tree and does not get a voicemail beep. They get a conversation and a confirmed appointment.

VAPITwilioElevenLabs HubSpot CRMn8nCalendar APIs

The problem

Staffing a front desk for round-the-clock coverage is expensive and inconsistent, and every missed call is a booking that went to a competitor instead. The client needed calls answered instantly, at any hour, without hiring for it. Answering services were the obvious alternative and the wrong one: they take a message, which just moves the work to the morning.

What we shipped

  • A voice agent that reads caller intent in real time
  • Live availability checks before a time slot is ever offered
  • Appointment booking with zero human handoff
  • Every call logged straight into HubSpot CRM
  • Graceful escalation when a call is genuinely out of scope

Where it stands today

  • Answering real inbound calls, 24 hours a day
  • No manual data entry after a booked call
  • No human required in the loop
  • One of several AI systems we run in production today
  • The same pattern is now being fitted to dispatch phone traffic
