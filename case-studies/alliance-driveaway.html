Trucking & Transport

What it is

The complete digital ecosystem for a North American vehicle relocation company: public website, internal dispatch dashboard, and native iOS and Android apps connecting 350+ drivers, clients and operations. Real-time dispatch, driver management, job tracking and client communication, with QuickBooks-integrated accounting underneath the whole thing. Built to Small Woman-Owned Business standards.

PHP / LaravelVue.jsSwift KotlinMySQLQuickBooksReal-time tracking

The problem

A distributed network of contractor drivers, one-way jobs spread across a continent, and no software on the market built for an operation where the vehicle being moved is the freight itself. Dispatch ran on phone calls, spreadsheets and email threads that drifted out of sync the moment a job changed hands. Every driveaway carrier we have talked to since describes the same failure: the schedule in the office and the schedule in the cab stop matching by mid-morning.

What we shipped

  • A live dispatch dashboard: job creation to delivery in one view
  • Native iOS and Android driver apps, offline-tolerant by design
  • Photo, GPS and signature chain-of-custody on every handoff
  • Deep QuickBooks sync: load to invoice with no retyping
  • Client-facing job visibility, so status calls stop coming in

Where it stands today

  • 350+ screened CDL drivers dispatched through the platform
  • 48 US states and Canada
  • Class 6, 7 and 8 equipment
  • Live truck tracking currently in build
  • Ongoing roadmap ownership, not a handover and goodbye
Live demo

Watch a load move through the board.

A simplified replay of the dispatch view. Step through it, or let it run. Every state change below is one a dispatcher actually sees.

Load Unit Lane Driver Status
ADS-4417Class 8 sleeper Edmonton, AB › Laredo, TX Unassigned Posted
ADS-4418Class 6 box Calgary, AB › Denver, CO M. Okafor In transit
ADS-4419Class 7 chassis Winnipeg, MB › Fargo, ND D. Whitecalf Delivered
  1. Load posted. ADS-4417 enters the board with the lane, equipment class and pickup window attached.
  2. Driver accepts. The job pushes to screened CDL drivers in range. First qualified acceptance takes it.
  3. Pickup verified. Driver captures photos, GPS and a signature. Chain of custody starts here, not at delivery.
  4. In transit. Position updates keep dispatch and the client honest without a single check-in call.
  5. Delivered. Second photo and signature set closes custody. The client sees it before the driver is off the lot.
  6. Invoiced. The load syncs to QuickBooks. Nobody retypes anything into accounting.
