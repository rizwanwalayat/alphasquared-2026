Trucking & Transport · Live in production

Alliance Driveaway
Solutions.

A North American Class 6/7/8 vehicle-relocation company. We built their entire digital ecosystem: a dispatch web dashboard plus native iOS and Android driver apps, keeping drivers, clients and operations in sync with real-time job tracking — with QuickBooks-integrated accounting underneath it.

PHP / LaravelVue.jsSwift KotlinQuickBooksReal-time tracking

The problem

A distributed network of contractor drivers, one-way jobs spread across a continent, and no software on the market built for an operation where the vehicle being moved is the freight itself. Dispatch ran on phone calls, spreadsheets and email threads that drifted out of sync the moment a job changed hands.

What we shipped

  • A live dispatch dashboard — job creation to delivery in one view
  • Native iOS and Android driver apps, offline-tolerant
  • Photo, GPS and signature chain-of-custody on every handoff
  • Deep QuickBooks sync — load to invoice with no retyping

Where it stands today

  • 350+ screened CDL drivers dispatched
  • 48 US states and Canada
  • Class 6, 7 and 8 equipment
  • Live truck tracking currently in build
Energy & Industrial · In active use

FluidsData.

An AI-powered analytics platform for the oil and gas sector. It turns raw, messy drilling-fluids and technical data into structured dashboards and exploratory analysis, and its Fluids Intelligence layer couples domain models with an agentic AI assistant for predictive guidance.

GolangPythonVue.jsMySQLAgentic AI

The problem

Field and lab data for fluids engineering arrives messy, siloed and slow to interpret — the kind of raw technical data that usually means someone spends hours in spreadsheets before a decision gets made, on wells where that delay has a real cost.

What we shipped

  • Structured dashboards over raw drilling-fluids data
  • Exploratory analysis tooling for fluids engineers
  • A Fluids Intelligence layer combining domain models with AI
  • An agentic assistant for predictive guidance, not just charts

Where it stands today

  • In active use by teams at Chevron
  • In active use by teams at ExxonMobil
  • In active use by teams at ADNOC
  • Built and maintained for a high bar on data trust
AI in production · Live in production

AI Front Desk.

An autonomous voice agent that answers real inbound calls around the clock — understanding intent, checking live availability, booking appointments and logging everything straight into the CRM. No human in the loop.

VAPITwilioElevenLabsHubSpot CRM

The problem

Staffing a front desk for round-the-clock coverage is expensive and inconsistent, and every missed call is a booking that went to a competitor instead. The client needed calls answered instantly, at any hour, without hiring for it.

What we shipped

  • A voice agent that understands caller intent in real time
  • Live availability checks before offering a time slot
  • Appointment booking with zero human handoff
  • Every call logged straight into HubSpot CRM

Where it stands today

  • Answering real inbound calls, 24 hours a day
  • No manual data entry after a booked call
  • No human required in the loop
  • One of several AI systems we run in production today
